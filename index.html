Where is my data stored?

Your budgets and expenses are stored locally on your device. They are not automatically sent to a server operated by the developer.

How can I prepare for a new device?

Use the in-app backup feature to export a JSON backup file, then keep it in a location you trust.

Can I protect a private budget?

Yes. Each budget can be protected with Face ID or your device passcode. You can also mask its details on the Home screen.

Does changing currency convert amounts?

No. Changing the currency changes the display format only. The app does not perform currency conversion.

Privacy Policy

Your data stays yours.

Last updated: August 21, 2026

1. Our approach

Ato Ikura (the “App”) respects your privacy. Data used for budget management is generally handled and stored locally on your device.

2. Information we collect

The App does not collect or transmit your name, email address, location, advertising identifier, usage history, or other personal data to a server operated by the developer. No account registration is required.

3. Data stored on your device

Budgets, expenses, budget additions, notes, and settings you enter are stored locally on your device. Deleting the App may delete this data unless you have saved a separate backup.

4. Backups and sharing

When you use the backup feature, your budgets and notes are exported to a JSON file. When you use the sharing feature, the information you choose is passed to the destination you select. These actions occur only when you initiate them. You are responsible for securely managing exported files and shared content.

5. Face ID and passcode authentication

The App uses authentication features provided by Apple. The App does not receive or store your face image, device passcode, or other biometric information.

6. Third parties and external services

The App does not include developer-operated advertising, analytics, or personal-data sharing with third parties. If you choose an external service through the system share sheet, that service’s privacy policy applies.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the App changes or when required by law. Material changes will be announced on this page or in the App when appropriate.
